Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial and nothing to unlock.

Does it submit applications for me?

No. HireFill fills the form and highlights what it filled. You review everything and click Submit yourself, so nothing goes out with a mistake.

Which sites does it work on?

Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Taleo, SuccessFactors, BambooHR, Jobvite and about 15 more applicant systems. On other career sites, click the HireFill icon or press the shortcut and it fills the standard fields it recognizes.

What about Workday's dropdown menus?

HireFill fills Workday's text fields, resume upload and skills picker. Some of Workday's custom dropdowns, like Country and State, still need a quick manual pick.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser's local extension storage on your own computer. You can export, edit or delete it anytime, and uninstalling removes it completely.
